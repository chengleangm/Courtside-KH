# Recommended Next Functions

The following additions would provide the most value after the current Option 1 system is approved.

1. **Blocked periods and private events manager**
   - Let admins block a court for maintenance, tournaments, private events or holidays directly from the calendar.

2. **Customer booking lookup**
   - Customers enter their booking reference and phone number to view, cancel or request a reschedule.

3. **Telegram confirmation and reminder automation**
   - Send booking confirmations, status changes and reminders to the customer and Courtside KH team.

4. **Payment tracking and receipts**
   - Record unpaid, deposit paid and fully paid statuses, payment method and receipt number.

5. **Daily check-in view**
   - A simple reception screen showing today’s upcoming bookings with Arrived, Playing and Completed buttons.

6. **Blocked-slot waitlist**
   - Customers can join a waitlist when a preferred time is booked and receive a message if it becomes available.

7. **Reports and CSV export**
   - Export bookings, revenue, court utilisation, cancellations and popular hours by date range.

8. **Staff accounts and permissions**
   - Separate permissions for administrators, reception staff, coaches and managers.

9. **Promotion codes and member pricing**
   - Discount codes, memberships, prepaid hours and different peak/off-peak prices.

10. **Audit history**
    - Record which staff member created, edited, confirmed, rescheduled or cancelled each booking.
